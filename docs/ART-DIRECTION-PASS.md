Branch `design/art-direction-pass`, started from `main` at 9c63c10 after the modernization merge.

**Status:** **deployed to production and checked there.** [PR #2](https://github.com/Ravonus/waambat.com/pull/2) was merged to `main` (5330475) on 12 Oct 2026 and Netlify deployed it to `waambat.netlify.app`. On that URL: all 11 content routes load at desktop and phone width with no horizontal overflow and no console errors (22 of 22 route and viewport combinations); the banner plays and advances on both; the wordmark leads home; the hero files are served with the one-hour cache and range support; `/admin` stays private and uncached; and the published headings and artwork ids on all 8 content routes are identical to before the deploy. ## Her visual language, and what it became

Before designing, every video on the site, LUX and Kismet Twist (recorded from the live inscriptions) and the interactive pieces were reviewed as frames and in motion. What carries across the work:

- A black ground. Colour is never a background; it comes from the artwork.
- Dense, saturated collage clusters that read as one creature or flower: faces, animals, cars, flames, hands, all upright and recognizable.
- A radial chromatic bloom around the bright centre, with red/blue fringing at the edges.
- Faces and bodies stretched into glossy ribbons; pixel-block mosaic that resolves into the image.
- A palette of pink, coral, crimson, amber and violet that is already in the site's tokens (`#ff94b2` wordmark, `#e1bcc2` headings, `#9457ff` links, `#ff7070` hover).

So the new motion is built from those, not from a preset: a stepped, slightly chromatic arrival (violet and coral fringe resolving to pink) for the wordmark and page titles; datamosh, mosaic and chromatic-displacement transitions in the banner; bloom and radial chroma in the montage; nothing rotated or restyled. No new colour, theme or font was added.

## Hero banner

A 24 second silent loop of her own works, rendered with the maintained Keel trailer plugin (not a disposable script). 35 source videos are used: 22 NFT paintings that still load, LUX and Kismet Twist, the art-archive videos, the bio animation, and the contact, mechanics and press videos (the three with embedded text only through text-free crops). The third mechanics video, whose Imgur file is gone, is the 26 Oct 2023 Wayback Machine copy of the original. Nine shots, each a main work with small satellites in screen/lighten blends (each satellite shows its whole piece, fitted inside a soft elliptical edge that fades into the black, and is kept inside the frame, so no piece is cut off or shows a hard rectangle), bridged by displacement, datamosh and mosaic transitions; the last shot hands over to the first so the loop has no visible join. No text is added over the artwork and no decorative shapes are drawn.

- `public/hero/waambat-hero-desktop.mp4` 1280×720 and `waambat-hero-mobile.mp4` 720×900, H.264, no audio, 3.72 MB and 2.77 MB (each under the studio's 4 MB upload cap, so she can replace them without a developer), plus a poster for each (the first frame).
- The wordmark is live text in the exact brand font and logo colour, set in the quiet left column (desktop) or top band (phones), clear of the artwork stage. A hero with an `logoSrc` image uses that instead.
- The wordmark is a link home. On the banner it is the link (the header wordmark is hidden there); on every other page the header wordmark is; and the phone menu's wordmark is too (from the home page it just closes the menu). In the studio's private preview it leads to the preview's home instead of leaving for the published site. Only the wordmark itself takes the click, so the banner's controls stay reachable.
- Home only: the banner fills the first screen under the navigation (`max-width` follows the video's own ratio, black sides on very wide screens, never cropped). The old header wordmark is hidden there so there is one wordmark, not two.
- Phones held upright get the portrait render, poster and composition. A phone on its side gets the landscape one, whole within the screen.
- Reduced motion and Save-Data show the poster until she presses play. A broken video shows an honest message and a retry control over the poster; nothing is hidden behind a placeholder.
- Playback follows the existing lifecycle: muted, looping, starts near the viewport, pauses offscreen, in a hidden tab, and while the mobile menu is open; a manual pause is respected through scrolling.

The whole montage is reproducible and editable in `keel/hero/` (`README.md` there has the details): composition generator, committed `timeline.json`, source provenance with hashes, fetch/capture/encode/render scripts, and the Keel plugin extensions it needs (`keel-trailer-art-montage.patch`, also pushed as branch `waambat-hero-materials` of `keel-web3/keel-trailer`, two commits on `63a55a7`, with their own tests; no pull request is open there yet).

## Ordinals page

Before: LUX opened as a bare spinner while its inscription assembled itself, Kismet Twist sat below it, the three early GIFs ran down the page in a left/right list, and the Gamma artwork was only a text link. Now:

- **One row of the three live pieces** (LUX, Kismet Twist and the Gamma artwork, _Edge Corporeal_) on a desktop, so all three are on screen without scrolling at 1512×733; one square column on a phone. Each is uncropped on black with a hairline and a faint pink glow; the title, one line about it and its links sit under it. The early GIF inscriptions (Ordinal A, B, C) follow at their native size under her ">>>>> click through any artwork... <<<<<" line, each a link to its inscription page.
- **Every piece appears at once.** Each has a cached loop (`public/previews/`, 10 s, 640×640, H.264, silent, 1.50 MB LUX, 0.95 MB Kismet, 1.51 MB Edge Corporeal) and a first-frame still (23 to 37 KB). The still is in the server-rendered page and is preloaded for the first two pieces; the loop starts as soon as the page is interactive, unless Save-Data or reduced motion is on (then it waits for Play), and it pauses when off-screen. No request goes to ordinals.com until a visitor asks for LUX. Each loop is cut so its tail crossfades into its head: it repeats with no jump.
- **Cached forever.** The file names carry a hash of their own bytes (`lux-9b87c251ff.mp4`, `lux-43c6cf7659.webp`), so a new render is a new URL, and `/previews/*` is served `public, max-age=31536000, immutable` (in `netlify.toml`, which is what applies on Netlify's CDN, and in `next.config.js`). A unit test fails if a file is not named by its own hash or a file in the folder is unreferenced.
- **Only LUX is interactive.** Kismet Twist and Edge Corporeal are videos (their inscriptions script an encoded video), so their loop is the artwork: a pause control, a link to the marketplace page, and "Original inscription" for the inscription itself. LUX has "Interact live": it mounts the live inscription over the loop, keeps the loop playing until LUX has drawn something (its own loader runs about 5.5 seconds after the document loads; measured cold and warm), then fades it in. The page never scrolls into a trapped frame: the live piece takes the pointer only while "Interact" is on (Escape or moving focus away turns it off), the document is kept when it leaves the screen or the phone menu opens, and "Back to preview" returns to the loop.
- **Edge Corporeal.** The title, artist (WAAMBAT) and technomancer (Ravonus) are in the inscription's own HTML and on-chain metadata, so the old "title and preview metadata have not been verified" placeholder is gone. The Gamma link is kept ("View on Gamma").
- **Her stored content.** `ordinalSourceVersion` is now 2. A store saved before this change is upgraded on read (never written until she saves): the loops are added only where none is set, the LUX/Kismet stills only where the still is empty or the old default, the captions "Live full LUX..." and "Live Kismet Twist #1..." only where they are exactly the old text, and the Gamma link becomes a piece only from its exact old default. Anything she has edited stays hers.
- **Studio.** Provider-player items have a new "Cached preview loop" field (a file upload up to 4 MB works too).

To remake the loops: capture LUX and Edge Corporeal with `keel/hero/capture-ordinals.mjs` (real time, on a GPU), Kismet with the Keel capture, then `sh keel/hero/encode-ordinal-previews.sh <lux.mp4> <kismet.mp4> <gamma-frames-dir>` and put the printed paths in `PREVIEWS` (`src/lib/ordinal-sources.ts`) and the seed.

## Editing it in the studio

The banner is an ordinary section with `Hero banner` placement, so the existing studio edits it, and saved content and her edits are untouched.

- Section title: the wordmark text. Hero item: video, mobile video, poster, mobile poster, optional logo image, width/height ratio, mobile ratio, archive. Archiving the section or the item removes the banner; moving it to another page moves it there.
- Theme: wordmark colour and font (as before), new "hero wordmark size" and "motion" (full, calm, off). The visitor's reduced-motion setting always wins.
- Content stored before the banner existed is shown with the default banner on read and never written back; her next ordinary Save persists it. An archived or edited banner is respected and never restored. The stored `heroVersion` field records this. Ids can only be archived, never removed, as before.
- Uploads reuse the existing uploader and its 4 MB limit and byte-signature checks; no auth or storage change.

## Polish across the site

- **Spacing:** the per-artwork gap from her original flow is unchanged on desktop and scaled to 40% on phones, where a 200px black gap read as a missing image.
- **Reveals:** artwork, captions and archive rows fade in and rise a short way as they are reached; caption titles resolve out of the violet/coral fringe; page titles arrive like the wordmark. They are scroll-driven CSS, so no script, no effect on the video lifecycle or layout, and they simply do not run where unsupported, with reduced motion, or with motion set to "off" ("calm" keeps the fade only).
- **Hierarchy:** captions keep the title strong and let year and medium recede; titles take the hover colour from the palette.
- **Navigation:** the underline for hover, focus and the current page carries the same violet/coral split; existing keyboard, touch, scroll-lock and history behaviour is unchanged.
- **Controls:** media controls and the LUX "Interact" gate get a logo-pink border/focus ring and a pressed state. The gate's behaviour is untouched.
- Left as designed: the archive mosaic, bio and Instagram character, the other pages' distinct layouts, the custom pink cursor, aspect ratios, source links and the LUX iframe and its state.

### Two checks that were failing, and why

Both failed on the unmodified base commit too, so they were not regressions, but they were not verifying anything either. Neither was a macOS problem.

- **`check-private-preview`** could not start: port 3031 is held on this Mac by launchd (pid 1), so its server never came up. The port is now `PREVIEW_PORT` (default unchanged). Once running, it correctly caught a real consequence of the banner: the first painting no longer starts at her original `y` of 419.7px. The assertion now keeps her column (left edge, 960px width, the painting-to-caption distance) and checks the first painting sits one banner plus her own 200px gap (238px in all) below the banner. It runs under reduced motion, so the 18px reveal rise is not in play.
- **`check-ordinal-presentation`** was stale. Commit 7565793 ("Retain LUX artwork state…") changed the inscription player to keep an activated document attached and removed the pause control, but this check still clicked "Pause Kismet Twist" and expected the frame to be removed off-screen. It was brought up to date, and has since been rewritten for the redesigned Ordinals page (see that section): loops playing from cached files with no request to ordinals.com, one row on a desktop, LUX the only interactive piece, reduced motion needing an explicit Play.

A note for anyone running the check scripts here: they stop `next start` but leave its `next-server` child running, which keeps the port. Kill leftovers (`pkill -f next-server`) before re-running on a fixed port.

## Remaining limits

- **Firefox was not run.** There is no Playwright Firefox build here and the installed Firefox hangs at launch in its own self-updater (which also raises a macOS install prompt), so it was stopped rather than worked around. What is covered instead: the WebKit run, and the no-scroll-animation probe above, which is the Firefox-relevant risk; the reveals are gated by `@supports`, so a browser without the feature gets the final state. Nothing about the banner depends on Firefox-specific behaviour (plain `<video>`, `<picture>`, container-query units), but it has not been seen there.
- **Hosted state.** Her saved edits live in the Netlify Blobs store, which cannot be read from outside the admin. What was verified: the live site's published content is identical to the seed on every content route (headings and artwork ids), and compatibility with an older-format store was exercised against a pre-banner seed in the local Git fixture. That does not prove the format of her stored draft. Saving the new fields on the hosted store needs an admin login, which only she or you can do; on the first save the banner is persisted from its default.
- **Cache header.** `/hero/*` is meant to cache for one hour with background revalidation. On the first deploy preview Netlify served it with its default `max-age=0, must-revalidate`, because Next's `headers()` does not apply to `public/` files that Netlify serves from its CDN. The rule is now also in `netlify.toml`, and production serves `public,max-age=3600,stale-while-revalidate=86400` with range support (checked on the production URL).
- **Source media.** Of the dead Imgur files, the third mechanics video (`TBObuJ0`) was recovered from the Wayback Machine (a 26 Oct 2023 snapshot of a file uploaded in February 2023) and is in the banner. _Autocorrection_ (`5oHMMfH`) and the first mechanics video (`3hhNUKR`) have no archived copy, so they are not in the banner and the site still shows them as unavailable. The provider-hosted archive videos cannot be downloaded. LUX needs a real GPU in real time to record; a recapture will differ in detail from the committed render.
- **Low-resolution sources.** The press strip (468x196 crop) and the second selfie (320x400) are small files, but they appear in the montage as small satellites at about 228x95 and 127x159 (desktop) and 269x113 and 100x124 (phone), below native size, so they are not upscaled.
- **Preview URL.** `netlify.toml` pins the deploy-preview `AUTH_URL` to `deploy-preview-1`, so signing in to the admin on `deploy-preview-2` is not expected to work.
- **Keel.** The plugin extensions are on `keel-web3/keel-trailer` branch `waambat-hero-materials` (00d3d09) and in `keel/hero/keel-trailer-art-montage.patch`; [keel-web3/keel-trailer#2](https://github.com/keel-web3/keel-trailer/pull/2) is open for review there.
